Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

Outrank India provides digital promotional placement and leaderboard visibility. Payments made for a completed placement are generally non-refundable once the placement has been successfully activated.

2. Before Payment

Users should review their listing information, selected amount and expected placement before completing payment. The applicable amount will be displayed before payment is completed.

3. Failed or Unsuccessful Payments

If a payment is unsuccessful or is not confirmed by the payment provider, the placement will not be activated. If an amount has been debited but the payment status is not successfully confirmed, the transaction may be reviewed and resolved through the applicable payment provider.

4. Duplicate Payments

If you believe you have been charged more than once for the same placement, please contact us with the transaction details. We will investigate verified duplicate transactions and process an appropriate resolution where applicable.

5. Cancellation

Once a paid placement has been successfully activated, users generally cannot cancel the placement for a refund. Users may contact us if there is an exceptional technical issue affecting the purchased service.

6. Incorrect Listing Information

Users are responsible for reviewing the information submitted before completing payment. Payments are generally not refundable solely because a user entered incorrect listing information.

7. Listing Removal

Outrank India may remove or suspend a listing that violates our Terms & Conditions or applicable law. Refund eligibility in such circumstances will be determined based on the circumstances of the case.

8. Contact

For refund or transaction-related questions, please contact us through the Contact Us page and include your listing name and transaction details where available.
